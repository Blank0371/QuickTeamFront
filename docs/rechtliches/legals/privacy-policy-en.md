# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated: 9 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how BlankTrading UG (haftungsbeschränkt) (the "Provider", "we" or "us") processes personal data in the QuickTeam application and related services (the "Service"). It is written for the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG), and other applicable national data-protection law.

## 1. Controller and allocation of roles

QuickTeam is a staff-scheduling tool used by businesses (each an "Employer") to organise their teams. Who is responsible for which data under data-protection law depends on the type of data:

- **Operational employee data** (shifts, availability, preferences, vacation, absences, working-time information, messages within a team): For this data, the **Employer** that invited you is the **controller** within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR and decides on the purposes and means of processing. In this respect we act as **processor** (Art. 28 GDPR) and process this data solely on the Employer's documented instructions. Questions about this data, and the exercise of your data-subject rights regarding it, should go **to your Employer first**.
- **Account data needed to run the Service itself** (login credentials, the link between your login and your profiles, security logs, support and bug reports you send us): For this data, **we are the controller**.

If you are unsure who to contact, write to us at blanktrading@web.de and we will help you or route your request to the right place.

## 2. What data we process

- **Account data:** email address and, optionally, phone number; an authentication record and a password managed by our authentication provider (we never see your password in plain text). A one-time confirmation code (OTP) may be sent by email to log you in.
- **Profile data:** first and last name, role(s), contract type, target/maximum hours, overtime balance, vacation entitlement, and membership in one or more businesses; selected language.
- **Operational data:** shift assignments and history, availability and shift preferences (preferred/avoid), vacation and absence requests, shift-swap and emergency-substitution records, announcements and messages, poll votes, checklist/task completion, and shift notes you send or receive within a business.
- **Push token:** where notifications are enabled, a device-specific push token and the platform (iOS/Android); see Section 4.
- **Support data:** bug and problem reports and the content you include in them.
- **Technical data:** information needed to operate and secure the Service, such as session identifiers, timestamps, and error logs.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR (e.g. health data). Please do not enter such data into free-text fields (such as messages, notes, or reasons for absence).

## 3. Purposes of processing and legal bases

- **Providing the Service and your scheduling features** – performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and/or the legitimate interests of your Employer in organising its workforce (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). In the employment context, the Employer's processing is additionally based on Sec. 26 BDSG (processing for the purposes of the employment relationship).
- **Operating accounts, authenticating you, and securing the Service** – legitimate interests in a safe, functioning service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Helping your Employer meet working-time and record-keeping obligations** – compliance with a legal obligation to which the Employer is subject (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
- **Optional features that rely on your choice** (e.g. linking a phone number, enabling push notifications) – consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time with effect for the future.
- **Handling support and bug reports** – legitimate interests in improving and fixing the Service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 4. Push notifications

The Service can send you push notifications (e.g. about new messages, shift changes, open postings, or reminders). This happens only if you allow push notifications on your device.

- If you allow notifications, the app obtains a device-specific **push token** and stores it together with the platform (iOS/Android) with us. You can disable delivery per notification type in the app settings and entirely in your device's system settings.
- To send notifications we use the **push service of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA)**, which forwards the notifications to the operating-system vendors' push infrastructure: **Apple Push Notification service (Apple Inc., USA)** on iOS and **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC, USA)** on Android. The content of a notification (e.g. a subject line or preview) passes through these services.
- **Legal basis:** your consent by enabling notifications (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and our and/or your Employer's legitimate interest in timely information (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For the associated transfer to the USA, see Section 6.
- **Local reminders:** independently of this, the app can schedule shift reminders entirely on your device. These fire without a network connection; no data is transmitted to us or third parties in the process.

## 5. Recipients and processors

We share your data only to the extent necessary to provide the Service:

- **People inside your business:** your Employer/manager can see the operational data needed to schedule you. Coworkers can see limited information (such as your name and shared shifts) only where your Employer has enabled that in the business's visibility settings; otherwise coworker details are restricted.
- **Processors (Art. 28 GDPR):**
  - **Supabase, Inc.** – hosting, database, and authentication. Processing takes place in a data-centre region within the European Union (Ireland, AWS eu-west-1). A data-processing agreement is in place.
  - **650 Industries, Inc. (Expo)** – sending push notifications (see Section 4).
- **Platform push infrastructure:** Apple Inc. (APNs) and Google LLC (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for delivering push notifications (see Section 4).
- **App stores:** when you download and run the app, Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) process data as controllers in their own right under their respective privacy policies.

We **do not sell your personal data**, do **not use it for advertising**, and do **not use** any third-party analytics or tracking services. We disclose data to authorities only where we are legally required to do so or where it is necessary to protect rights and safety.

## 6. International transfers

The Service's hosting and database are operated within the EU/EEA (see Section 5).

In connection with **push notifications**, personal data may be transferred to services in the **USA** (Expo/650 Industries, Apple, Google). For these transfers we rely on appropriate safeguards under Chapter V GDPR, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) and – where the respective recipient is certified – on an adequacy decision based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). Where any further processing takes place outside the EEA, it likewise occurs only on the basis of such appropriate safeguards. You can obtain a copy of, or information about, the safeguards relied upon by contacting blanktrading@web.de.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- **Operational data** is retained for as long as the Employer's account is active and as needed for their working-time and record-keeping obligations; the Employer, as controller, decides on its retention and deletion.
- **Account data** is kept while your account exists and for a limited period afterwards as needed for security and legal purposes, then deleted or anonymised.
- **Push tokens** are deleted when you disable notifications, when the device reports the token as invalid, or when your account is deleted.
- **Support/bug reports** are deleted once they are no longer needed for handling them.
- You can delete your account within the app. When an employee record is deleted or anonymised, identifying fields (name, email, login link) are removed so that the record can no longer be attributed to you.

## 8. No automated individual decision-making

The Service includes a feature that automatically **generates proposed shift plans**. This serves as support only; the results are reviewed and approved by a responsible person (manager/Employer). There is **no solely automated decision** that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you (Art. 22 GDPR). We do not carry out profiling within the meaning of the GDPR.

## 9. Local storage on your device

To operate the Service, the app stores certain data locally on your device, in particular your login session, an offline read cache (e.g. calendar data), and the version of this Privacy Policy you have accepted. This data remains on your device and is removed when you log out or uninstall the app.

## 10. Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions, you have the right to:

- **access** the data processed about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
- **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
- **erasure** ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17 GDPR);
- **restriction** of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
- **data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR);
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

Where processing is based on **consent**, you may **withdraw** it at any time with effect for the future; the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal remains unaffected.

To exercise rights over **operational data**, contact your **Employer** (the controller); for **account data**, contact us at blanktrading@web.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a **data-protection supervisory authority** (Art. 77 GDPR). Our competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg (Germany); you may also contact the supervisory authority of your habitual residence.

## 11. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, row-level access controls that isolate each business's data, server-enforced authorisation, and least-privilege access. No system is perfectly secure; we work continuously to protect your information and to respond appropriately to incidents.

Certain security measures for the underlying infrastructure – including physical data-centre security, network protection, and encryption at rest – are provided on our behalf by Supabase acting as our processor under a data-processing agreement, and Supabase is responsible for the security of the hosting infrastructure it operates. This does not relieve us of our own responsibilities: we remain responsible under the GDPR for the processing we carry out and for the security measures within our control, and we have selected a processor that provides sufficient guarantees of appropriate technical and organisational measures.
